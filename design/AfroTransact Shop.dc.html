About AfroTransact

Home is a flavour, a fabric, a sound. We deliver it.

AfroTransact is a marketplace built for diaspora communities — the grocers, tailors, braiders, caterers and makers who keep culture alive far from home, and the families who shop with them.

Photo: a seller’s shelf in Houston
{{ p.title }}{{ p.text }}

Our story

It started with a search for ofada rice in Houston that ended in a two-hour drive. The shops existed — they just weren’t online, couldn’t ship, or couldn’t take a card from someone three states away.

So we built the rails: verified sellers, one checkout across many shops, our own warehouses and drivers, and a team that reviews every business before it sells. We started with African communities in the USA and now welcome every diaspora.
