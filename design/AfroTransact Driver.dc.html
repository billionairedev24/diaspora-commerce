AfroTransact Delivery · driver app

Sign in, inspect, load, deliver — every step scanned and synced.

  1. Sign in + MFA. Access is checked against the permission delivery:operate (Driver role, or granted directly to e.g. an ops admin). Revoking it in Backoffice → Access control signs the driver out live.
  2. Pre-trip inspection. 10-point DVIR, odometer, fuel, cold-box temperature (required for perishables, 33–40°F) and 4 photos. A failed critical item blocks the route and alerts dispatch.
  3. Load-out scan. Every shipment label from the FC manifest is scanned (Code 128). Unknown or duplicate labels are rejected.
  4. Stops. Seller pickups: scan each label + handover name. Drop-offs: scan, photo and the customer’s one-time code.
  5. End of shift. Failed packages are scanned back into the FC; odometer must increase.

Each action is an event with an idempotency key in an on-device outbox. Offline, events queue; online, they sync in order to order-service (actor: AfroTransact Delivery). Watch them in Backoffice → Delivery dispatch.
