Sellers and service providers run their business from **Seller Center**.

## Your home page

Home shows one section per area you can open: **Sales**, **Orders**, **Listings**, **Payouts**, **Inventory**,
**Reviews**, **Account health** and **Promotions** (service providers see **Bookings**, **Revenue**, **Availability**,
**Payouts** and **Account health**). Each section has one headline number with its period, a small chart, a line of
totals and up to three quick actions. Pick **7**, **30** or **90 days** at the top; we remember your choice. A section
you have not set up yet shows a short prompt with one button (for example **Add your first product**) instead of
zeros. Sections your role or plan cannot open are not shown. Exportable reports (PDF, Excel, CSV) sit below the
sections.

## 1. Apply to sell

1. Sign in and choose **Sell on AfroTransact**. Pick whether you sell products, offer services, or both.
2. Complete each step: business details, owners (not needed for sole proprietors), compliance documents, payout
   account, and plan. Your progress is saved automatically.
3. Choose **Submit for review**.

### What the status on your application means

| Status you see | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | You have not submitted yet. | Finish the steps and submit. |
| **In review** | Our team is checking your application. | Nothing. We email you when it moves. |
| **Needs your attention** (returned to you) | We need a document or a correction. The message and the list of requested items are on top of the application. | Fix those items, then press **Resubmit for review**. |
| **Approved** | You can sell. | Add listings. |
| **Not approved** | We could not approve the application; the reason is shown. | Read the note; contact support if you think it is a mistake. |

:::info "In review" can last longer than you expect
An application can be checked more than once (for example a second approver confirms a first reviewer's decision, or
a reviewer escalates a check to a senior colleague). You only see **In review** during all of this. If we need
anything from you, the status changes to **Needs your attention** and you will get an email.
:::

## 2. Add listings

**Listings** → *New listing*: title, photos, price, stock, shipping options and category. Food and some regulated
categories need a permit or licence uploaded in your application. Services (**Services** menu) need duration,
price, hours and a calendar.

### What each category asks for

The product form asks only what your category needs, and says so at the top: **What you need to list this product**, a
checklist where every item opens its field. Chips next to the category tell you whether you can **Sell this product**
straight away, **Apply to sell** once (then every listing in that category or brand goes live), or whether it is **Not
available**.

- **Photos.** At least one picture. Packaged food, baby feeding, beauty, health products and pet food also need a
  **label photo** (the ingredients and nutrition panel, the Supplement Facts or the Drug Facts). It is a slot of its own;
  other pictures do not count. For packaged groceries you do not type the ingredients: the label photo covers it, and you
  can tick the allergens it contains. Prepared food (cooked or baked by you) asks for typed ingredients and the allergen
  list instead.
- **Package size and weight.** Every physical product needs the length, width, height and weight of the box as shipped
  (at most 108 in on each side and 150 lb; you can type cm and kg). Give each size or colour its own box when they
  differ: a variant you leave empty starts from the first one, and **Same package for all variants** copies it down.
  Digital products, gift cards and services have no package.
- **Country of origin** for every physical product, and the **dangerous goods** question (aerosols, flammables,
  batteries, candles, cleaners) where it applies. A *yes* keeps the item out of Fulfilled by AfroTransact until we review
  it.
- **Condition** (New, Renewed, Used) is asked only for electronics, books, general goods and toys. Everything else is
  listed as New, and car seats are always New.
- **Product facts**: net weight, servings, shelf life or expiry, storage, material, model number, age grade. Where a
  question says *required*, you must answer it. Where it can be left out, tick **I can't provide this** and write why (for
  example "handmade, no model number").
- **Barcode**: required where the category says so; handmade and unbranded items may say they have none.
- **Documents** (permit, brand authorization, safety or lab certificate, insurance, purchase invoice): upload them in
  the **Compliance** section. They are kept private and reviewed by the approval team when you apply for a category or
  brand; they never hold up a listing.

The bulk upload template is built from the same rules: pick a category, download its template, and the columns, the
required stars and the help text match what the form asks. Rows are checked the same way, with a plain message on the
column that needs fixing; the package columns are per row (leave them empty on a variant that ships in the first row's
box).

### Clothes and shoes: sizes and size charts

For fashion categories you can write your own sizes ("UK 12", "Free size", "EU 38") and colours ("Indigo adire") when
ours do not fit; "XL" and "xl" count as one. Write a **size chart** once (**Listings → Size charts**): pick cm or
inches, enter each size's measurements as a number (`88`) or a range (`86-90`), say how to measure and whether your
sizes run small, true to size or large. Then choose the chart on each product. Every size you sell needs a row in the
chart, and some categories require a chart. Shoppers see the chart beside the size picker with a cm / in switch.
You can also write or change a chart without leaving the product form (**Size guide** section). Charts can be uploaded
from a spreadsheet (**Listings → Size charts → Import charts**): download the template, fill one row per size, then
**Check the file**; nothing is saved until you import, each chart is saved whole or not at all, and a name you already use
updates that chart. A chart you retire keeps showing on products that already use it.

### Which departments you sell in

You choose your **departments** (Groceries, Fashion, Beauty and so on) when you apply, and AfroTransact records them when
your application is approved. From then on Seller Center shows only those departments: in the product form, the bulk
upload and its template, brand choices and the size chart pages. **Size charts** appears under Listings only when one of
your departments sells clothing or shoes. The same rule is enforced on our side, so a listing, draft, upload row or size
chart in another department is refused with a plain message that tells you how to add it.

Need another department? At the bottom of the category list in the product form choose **Don't see your category? Request a
department** (or use **Departments** on the Preferences page), pick the department and send. AfroTransact reviews the request
and, when it is approved, the department is added to your store at once. The answer tells you what that department also
needs, for example confirming full ingredient labelling for Beauty. Groceries needs no food permit; a permit is only asked
of food services such as catering. Listings you already have stay live and you can keep editing their price, stock and text.

### Service providers: your service area

Services are in person only. In **Services**, set your **service area** first: your base address, **how far you travel to
customers** (your radius, within the minimum and maximum for your service categories) and, if you want, extra cities, ZIP
codes or whole metro zones such as Greater Austin. The preview shows the cities your radius covers.

Your radius matters for services that **come to the customer** (home visits, catering): you are shown only to customers
inside it, because you cannot travel past it. For services where the customer **goes to you** (your salon or shop), shoppers
choose how far they are willing to go, so your radius never limits them: the closer your shop, the higher you appear (results
are nearest first).
For each service choose where it happens: **Comes to you** (home visits, catering), **You go to them** (your salon or
shop), or both, add a category, and add a photo of your work (without one, customers see your initials).

## 3. Fulfil orders

New orders appear in **Orders**. Ship with USPS, UPS or FedEx, add tracking, and mark as shipped. Customers are
notified automatically. Use **Fulfilled by AfroTransact** to send stock to us and let us pick, pack and ship.

## 4. Returns, disputes and account health

- **Returns** and **Disputes** list cases needing your response; answer before the due date shown.
- **Account health** shows the metrics we monitor (late shipments, cancellations, disputes). Poor health can limit selling.

## Reviews

**Reviews** shows what customers who received your products say, with the product, the stars, photos and the reviewer's
first name and last initial. Reply **once** to any review: the reply is public, shown under the review, and cannot be edited
afterwards. You cannot edit, hide or delete a review. If one breaks the rules (abusive, personal information, spam, not about
the product, written by a competitor) use **Report**; Trust & Safety decide and the review stays up until they do. Only your
Listings access is needed to see Reviews.

## 5. Team

**Team** lets owners invite colleagues with a role (for example orders-only access). Invitations arrive by email.
