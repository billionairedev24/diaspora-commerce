## How you get paid

Customer payments are held by our payment provider (Stripe). After an order is eligible, your share (sale price
minus fees and refunds) becomes available and is paid to your bank account through **Stripe Connect**. The
**Earnings** page shows each order's gross, fees, tax and net; **Payouts** shows each payout and its status.

| Payout status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Requested / Processing** | We have created it and are paying it. |
| **In transit** | Stripe has sent it to your bank; banks usually take a few business days. |
| **Paid** | Your bank has received it. |
| **Failed** | The bank rejected it (wrong or closed account). Fix your bank details; we retry. |

### My payout is missing or late

1. Open **Payouts** and find it. Check the status above.
2. **In transit** is normal for several business days.
3. **Failed**: update the bank account under *Payouts → Bank account* (Stripe may ask you to verify again).
4. Still stuck? Contact support with the payout reference; staff can look it up directly in Stripe.

If Stripe needs more information from you (identity, address, bank proof), Seller Center shows a banner with a link
to finish. Payouts pause until it is done.

## Fees

Your plan sets your subscription and the marketplace commission. **Billing** shows your plan, invoices and
the fee schedule. Fees are deducted from each order before payout, never charged separately without an invoice.

## Tax

- AfroTransact calculates **sales tax on the customer's order** using the delivery address and the product's tax
  category, and shows it at checkout. On marketplace sales we collect and remit it where the law requires it.
- You provide your **tax ID** (EIN/SSN or equivalent) in your application. It is stored encrypted and only the
  last four digits are shown.
- Resale / exemption certificates: upload them under your application documents if you buy wholesale.
- **Sales tax statement**: *Payouts → Sales tax statement* shows, for each month, the tax collected on your orders by state, what refunds took back and the net, with a CSV to download for your books.
- **Tax documents** (such as year-end forms) are issued from the information in your account, so keep your legal
  name, address and tax ID current.

:::caution
This guide is not tax advice. Ask your accountant about your own obligations.
:::
