## Shoppers

**Where is my order?** *Your orders* shows live tracking. Carrier updates can take a few hours to appear.

**How do I return something?** *Account → Returns & refunds*. Most items: 30 days. Food only if damaged or wrong.

**When do I get my refund?** After the return is received or the claim is approved, to your original payment method; your bank may take a few days to show it.

**Why was I charged tax?** Sales tax depends on the delivery address and the item's tax category. It is itemised at checkout.

**Can I change my delivery address after paying?** Only before the seller ships. Contact the seller from the order, or support.

## Shoppers and sellers

**The site says "We'll be right back".** We are doing planned work. Your cart, orders and payments are safe. The page checks by itself and reloads when we are back.

## Sellers

**Why does my application still say "In review"?** It can pass through more than one reviewer. If something is needed, the status changes to *Needs your attention* with a message and you get an email.

**It says "Needs your attention". What now?** Read the message and the requested items at the top of the application, fix them, then press **Resubmit for review**. Nothing else is needed.

**Can I edit an application after submitting?** Not while it is in review or approved. Only when it is returned to you.

**My application was not approved. Can I reapply?** Read the reason; if it can be fixed, contact support to reopen it.

**Why do I need a tax ID, and is it safe?** Tax rules require it. It is stored encrypted and masked to the last four digits.

**Where is my payout?** See [Getting paid](./payouts-and-tax.md#my-payout-is-missing-or-late).

**What does a payout "Failed" mean?** The bank refused it. Correct your bank details and we retry.

**Why is the amount lower than the order total?** Fees, refunds and tax collected for the authorities are deducted; *Earnings* shows the breakdown per order.

**How do I invite a teammate?** *Team → Invite*, pick a role. They accept from the email.

## Staff

**I approved an application but it did not finish.** The final approver must be a different person than the reviewer.

**The seller was flagged but never told anything.** That is intended: a flag is internal. Use **Return to applicant** if they must fix something.

**"Check with Stripe" says it is not possible.** The payout is already in a final state; refresh the sheet.

**I get "no access" on a page.** Your role lacks the permission; ask an admin.

**How do I correct the ledger?** Create an adjustment; a second person approves it. Never edit entries.
