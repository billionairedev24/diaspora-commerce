## Buy something

1. Search or browse the storefront. Filter by market, category, price and delivery option.
2. Open a product or service page and add it to your cart (services are booked for a date and time instead).
3. Check out: confirm the delivery address or pickup option, review the tax shown, and pay.
4. You get an order confirmation by email and in **Your orders**.

## Track an order

Open **Your orders** (`/orders`). Each shipment shows live status from the carrier or AfroTransact Delivery.
AfroTransact Delivery drops come with a photo and a one-time code you give the driver.

## Rate an item you received

Once an item is delivered, **Your orders** and the order page offer **Rate this item** (so does the customer app). Give
1 to 5 stars and, if you like, a title, your thoughts (up to 4000 characters) and up to 5 photos. Only customers who
received the item can review it. Your review is shown as **Verified purchase** with your first name and last initial only.

- You can have one review per product. Edit it within 30 days; you can delete it at any time.
- Reviews with a link, an e-mail address, a phone number or offensive words are checked by our team before they are shown.
- The product page shows the average, the number of ratings, how many gave each star rating, and the reviews. Sort them
  by most recent, highest or lowest, or tap a star row to see only those. **Report** a review that breaks our rules.

## Return an item and get a refund

1. Go to **Account → Returns & refunds**.
2. Choose the order and items, and tell us why.
3. We email a label or arrange a pickup.
4. When the return is received (or a claim is approved) the refund goes to your **original payment method**.

Most items can be returned within 30 days. Food is refundable if damaged or wrong.

## Book a service

Choose the provider, a time slot, and any options. You can reschedule or cancel from **Your orders** within the
provider's cancellation policy, which is shown before you pay.

## Something went wrong

- **Not delivered / wrong item:** open the order and start a return or claim.
- **Dispute with a seller:** use *Report a problem* on the order; staff step in if you and the seller cannot agree.
- **Anything else:** chat with Lola (the assistant) any time, or contact support on the Help Center page.
