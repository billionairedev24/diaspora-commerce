Every domain event on the bus is documented in AsyncAPI 3: each service owns a fragment in
`contracts/asyncapi/fragments/<service>.yaml` and `tools/contracts/merge_asyncapi.py` merges them into
`contracts/asyncapi/events.yaml`. Topics are `at.<aggregate>.events`, keyed by aggregate id; headers carry
`event-id`, `event-type` and `occurred-at`. Producers publish through a transactional outbox and consumers
de-duplicate on `event-id`.

Producers and consumers come from the `send` / `receive` operations of each fragment. An event type listed
without a producer is consumed by a service whose producer has not published its fragment yet.
