# Returns, Refunds and Problems With Your Order

## The short version

- **Before your order is delivered:** you can cancel it yourself until the shipment has been prepared. You get your money back, or the charge is released.
- **After your order is delivered:** there is no return or refund button. If something is wrong, go to **Account > Help**, tell us what happened and add photos. Our team looks at every request and decides the outcome.
- **Refunds go back to whoever paid, the way they paid:** the card part to the card, the store credit part back as store credit.
- **Your legal rights stay.** Nothing on this page takes away rights you have under the law.

## Cancelling before delivery

You can cancel an order in Account > Orders until its shipment has been prepared. "Prepared" means we or the seller have picked and packed the items for dispatch. The order page shows whether cancelling is still possible. If the Cancel option is no longer shown, the package is on its way.

- **If you cancel in time,** the whole order total is returned, including delivery fee and tax. If your card was only authorised and not yet charged, the authorisation is released, so you are not charged and the hold falls off your statement in a few days (your bank decides how long). If the money was already collected, we refund it.
- **Orders with more than one package** are handled package by package. You can cancel a package that has not been prepared even if another package has.
- **Pre-orders and delivery runs** can be cancelled in the same way until the shipment is prepared. If we cancel a pre-order window or a run, every order on it is refunded in full, including the delivery fee, automatically. See the Pre-order and Delivery Run Terms.
- **Services and bookings** follow the cancellation policy shown by the provider before you booked. If the provider cancels or does not show up, you get a full refund, including any deposit.
- **Gift cards** are delivered by email and cannot be cancelled after they are sent. See the Gift Card Terms.

## After delivery: tell us what went wrong

When an order has been delivered, there is no self-service return or refund. Instead:

1. Go to **Account > Help** (or open the order and choose **Get help with this order**).
2. Choose the order, and the package if there is more than one.
3. Choose what went wrong: *I didn't receive it*, *It arrived damaged*, *I got the wrong item*, *It's not as described*, or *Something else*.
4. Describe what you expected and what you got, in your own words.
5. Add photos. JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC photos are accepted. Photos of the item, the packaging, any label and anything that shows the problem help us decide faster. For items that did not arrive, a photo of the delivery location or any delivery notice helps.
6. Send the request. You get a reference number, and we reply on your help requests page and by your notification channels.

Please report a problem as soon as you see it. For perishable food, report it on the day it arrives, and keep the item and packaging until we reply, unless it is unsafe to keep. Our team reviews your description, your photos, the delivery record (including the delivery code and proof of delivery), the seller's information and the order history. We may ask you for more detail or more photos. We may speak to the seller or the carrier. We aim to reply within [[LEGAL: Q32 reply target in business days, to be set by Support operations]]. Request statuses are Received, In review, Resolved, Closed or Withdrawn, and you can withdraw a request at any time.

**What we can decide.** Depending on the problem, our team may refund all or part of the order, replace the item, send a missing item, give you store credit, or decide that no refund is due. We will tell you our decision and the reason. You do not have to send items back unless we ask; if we do ask, we will pay for the return shipping and tell you how. Where an item is perishable, unsafe or not worth the cost of collecting, we may let you keep or dispose of it.

**What we need from you.** The facts must be true. We may refuse requests that are false, that come from a pattern of abuse, or for problems that are not covered, for example a change of mind, a wrong size you ordered, damage after delivery, or an item you used. We may take account of how often an account asks for help. Repeated false claims can lead to a limit or closure of the account under the Conditions of Use.

## How refunds are paid

A refund goes back to each person or method that paid, in the same way they paid.

- **The card (or other cash payment method) part** of the order goes back to that card or method. We cannot refund a card payment to a different card, to cash or to a person. Bank and card processing times apply: card refunds usually show up on your statement 5 to 10 business days after we issue them; bank (ACH) refunds can take up to 7 business days. [[LEGAL: Q13 confirm the refund timing statements]]
- **The store credit part** of the order goes back as store credit, with the same value, to the account that used it. See the Store Credit Terms.
- **A gift card used to pay** was turned into store credit when it was redeemed, so that part is returned as store credit.
- **If more than one person paid,** each payer gets back what they paid.
- **Delivery fees and tax** are included when the whole order is refunded. For a partial refund we say what is covered.
- **Membership fees** have their own refund rule in the Plus Membership Terms.

You cannot choose to have the card part refunded as credit, and the product has no such option. Only our staff, inside a help decision and with a recorded reason, may send the card part to your store credit instead of the card. They might do so, for example, if the card is closed and the refund would be returned. This is one way: credit is never turned back into a card refund. If a card refund fails, we will contact you to agree what to do.

## Credit as a goodwill gesture

Staff may add store credit to your account as a goodwill gesture or to settle a request, with a reason that is recorded. That credit follows the Store Credit Terms: it can only buy products and services and can never be turned into cash.

## Items you should not use or open

Perishable food, opened beauty and personal-care items, custom-made clothing and gift cards are not good candidates for a replacement, because they cannot safely be resold. We will still look at a request if one of these arrives damaged, defective, unsafe, not as described, or does not arrive.

## Recalls and safety

If an item you bought is recalled or found unsafe, we will tell you what to do. You will be refunded for recalled items as required by the recall or by the law.

## Your legal rights

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under federal or state law that cannot be waived, including rights about goods that are defective, not as described, not delivered, or delivered late, and any right to a refund or replacement under a statute, a card-network rule or a manufacturer's warranty. You may also ask your card issuer or bank to look at a charge, as the issuer's rules allow, and you can contact your state attorney general or consumer protection office. We ask you to contact us first, because we can usually fix a problem quickly. [[LEGAL: Q13 and Q23 verify this policy and the required disclosures against the refund-policy and consumer-protection laws of each launch state, FTC Mail, Internet and Telephone Order Merchandise Rule requirements, and the sale of perishables]]

Some goods come with a manufacturer's or seller's warranty. If a seller offers its own return or warranty terms on a listing, those are extra rights; they do not reduce your rights here.

## Services

Refunds for services follow the provider's cancellation policy shown before booking. If a provider cancels or does not show up, you get a full refund, including any deposit. If a service was not performed as promised, use Account > Help in the same way as for a product.
