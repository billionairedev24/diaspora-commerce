## 1. Agreement and how these terms fit together

1.2 They incorporate, and you also accept, the Seller Terms (for the sections on accounts, onboarding review, reserves and offsets, taxes, prohibited conduct, intellectual property, data protection, suspension, indemnity, liability, governing law and changes), the Payment Terms, the Data Processing Terms, the Seller Fees Summary and the Sub-processors list. In this document, "Seller Terms" sections apply to Providers as if "Seller" meant "Provider", "Order" included "Booking", and "Hold Window" meant the hold after service completion. If these terms conflict with the Seller Terms about services, these terms control. The Payment Terms control on money movement.

1.3 Definitions: "Service" is anything you offer for booking, whether in person, at the customer's home or virtual. "Booking" is a customer's reservation of a Service at a time. "Customer" is the person booking. "Practitioner" is an individual who performs a Service for you (an owner, employee or contractor). "Provider Hub" is your back office. "Deposit" is the part of the price charged to confirm a Booking. "Balance" is the remainder.

## 2. Eligibility, onboarding and regulated services

2.1 The two-step onboarding review in the Seller Terms applies. For services we also collect licence and insurance information for regulated services (such as licence number, issuing state, expiry date, licence document and proof of insurance), and we may decline categories we do not support.

2.2 Services that require a licence, certification, registration or permit (for example beauty and personal-care licences, health or wellness practice, food preparation, childcare, transportation, immigration, tax or legal services where permitted) may be offered only if you and each Practitioner hold, and keep in force, all required authorisations. You must tell us immediately of any lapse, suspension, restriction or complaint to a licensing body.

2.3 Some services are not permitted or need our approval (see section 13 and the Prohibited & Restricted Items policy). Services that Stripe prohibits or restricts for payment processing (see the Payment Terms) may not be offered, and offering them is a breach.

2.4 You must operate in a live market and service area as we notify you.

## 4. Services and listings

4.1 You describe each Service accurately: what is included, duration, price, deposit, mode (in person, at home or virtual), add-ons, preparation required, who performs it, location, cancellation policy and any restrictions (age, health conditions, allergies). Bookings copy the Service details at the time of booking, so later changes do not affect existing Bookings.

4.2 Listings are moderated as in the Seller Terms. We may hide or remove Services at any time for policy, safety or risk reasons.

4.3 You must not misrepresent qualifications or results, use before-and-after claims you cannot substantiate, or make medical or disease-treatment claims unless lawful and approved.

4.4 Content that Customers or you upload (photos, notes) is subject to the Intellectual Property sections of the Seller Terms.

## 5. Practitioners and team members

5.1 You may add team members to Provider Hub with roles (owner, manager, practitioner, finance). Each Practitioner has their own working hours and time off. Practitioners see only their own calendar unless given broader permission.

5.2 You are responsible for your team members as for yourself: their licences, insurance, background checks where required, training, wages and benefits, taxes and conduct. They are your employees or contractors, never ours. Misclassification and employment compliance are your responsibility.

5.3 You must ensure that team members who use Provider Hub follow our security rules, only access data they need, and are removed when they leave.

5.4 We may ask you to remove or replace a Practitioner whose conduct or authorisations create risk.

## 6. Availability, bookings and confirmation

6.1 You keep your availability current: working hours, time off, notice period, booking horizon, buffers and daily limits. Slots shown to Customers are generated from them. We try to prevent double booking but you are responsible for your calendar.

6.2 Instant bookings are confirmed automatically. For booking requests, you must accept or decline within the response time shown (at the time of drafting, four hours, but it is configured by Staff). If you do not respond the request expires and any hold on the Customer's payment is released.

6.3 Once you confirm a Booking you must perform the Service at the agreed time and place. Reschedule requests must be handled promptly; a proposed new time requires the Customer's acceptance.

6.4 Do not take bookings you cannot honour. Bookings you cancel count against your standing.

## 7. Prices, deposits and balance

7.1 The Customer's payment is authorised when they book. The Deposit is captured when the Booking is confirmed. The Balance is captured on completion or, for bookings far in the future, charged on the Customer's saved card shortly before the appointment (at the time of drafting, two days before) by Stripe on our behalf, with the Customer's consent obtained at booking. If the balance cannot be charged, the Booking may be flagged as unpaid and you may be allowed to cancel it.

7.2 You may not request payment from Customers outside the Marketplace for a Service booked on it, and you may not charge Customers extra fees not disclosed at booking.

7.3 A Service with no Deposit is charged in full on the same schedule as a Balance.

7.4 Prices are in US dollars and include everything except tax, which we calculate, collect and remit where required under the Seller Terms. [[LEGAL: confirm sales tax treatment of services by state and whether AfroTransact collects any tax on service bookings]]

## 8. Cancellation, rescheduling and no-show

8.1 Policies. You choose a cancellation policy for your Services from those we offer, such as Flexible, Moderate, Strict or Custom (a free-cancellation window, an optional partial refund, a no-show rule and a reschedule window and limit). The policy that applied when the Booking was made stays with that Booking. The Customer sees the exact refund before they cancel.

8.2 The standard policies work as follows at the time of drafting, and Staff may update them: Flexible, free cancellation until 24 hours before; Moderate, free until 48 hours before and half the Deposit kept between 24 and 48 hours; Strict, free until 7 days before. Custom policies must be within the limits we allow.

8.3 Customer cancellation. If the Customer cancels outside the free window, the part of the Deposit that your policy says you keep is paid to you (less fees) after the hold. Everything else is refunded.

8.4 No-show. If the Customer does not attend, you may mark a no-show after the start time. Your policy decides whether you keep the Deposit, or the full price, or nothing. You must mark no-shows honestly and in good time; false no-shows are fraud. A Customer may dispute a no-show through support, and Staff decide using the evidence.

8.5 Provider cancellation. If you cancel or do not attend, the Customer receives a full refund including any Deposit. A late cancellation by you, or a failure to attend, may incur a provider late-cancellation fee and affects your standing. The fee amount and timing are configured by Staff and shown in Provider Hub. We may recover the fee from your balance or your connected account.

8.6 Customer cancellation by us. We may cancel a Booking for fraud, safety, legal or operational reasons. Refunds follow what is fair; we may also refund Customers if your conduct caused the problem and recover the cost from you.

8.7 Completion. A Booking is completed when you mark it complete or, if you do not, automatically after a period after the appointment (at the time of drafting, 48 hours) unless the Customer has raised a problem.

8.8 Refunds for poor service. A Customer who is unhappy with a completed Service may raise a help request with evidence. Staff decide the outcome as set out in the Seller Terms (support and evidence disputes) and may refund all or part of the price. Any refund attributable to you is recovered from you as in the Seller Terms.

## 9. Fees: plan fee and connection fee

9.1 You pay a plan fee (if your plan has one) and a connection fee. The connection fee is a percentage of the service money that AfroTransact actually captures for you, charged when it is captured. The percentage is configured by Staff and can depend on your plan, the service category and rates negotiated with you in writing. The Seller Fees Summary explains how this works. Rates are not fixed in these terms.

9.2 Timing: the connection fee applies to the Deposit when captured, to the Balance when captured, and to a Deposit or no-show amount you keep after a late cancellation or no-show. Nothing is charged when a Booking is cancelled or declined before anything is captured. On a refund, the connection fee is returned in proportion where the fee schedule says so; otherwise AfroTransact keeps it.

9.3 Provider Hub shows an estimate of what you keep before you accept a request: the price less the connection fee and estimated card fees. Estimates are not guarantees.

9.4 Plan fees and the billing waterfall work as set out in the Seller Terms.

9.5 AfroTransact Retail does not pay a connection fee. This does not affect yours.

## 10. Payouts

10.1 Payouts are made through Stripe as set out in the Payment Terms and the Seller Terms. For a Service, the hold window starts when the Booking is completed (or a kept Deposit or no-show amount is settled), and lasts for the default period or the period Staff configure. After the hold, the net amount (price captured less connection fee and allocated card fees) is transferred to your connected account.

10.2 Payouts can be held, reserved, reduced or offset as in the Seller Terms (holds, reserves, offsets, chargebacks and negative balances), including for open complaints, disputes, chargebacks, late-cancellation fees and licensing lapses.

10.3 You are responsible for your own income tax, self-employment tax and registration. We report payments as required.

## 11. Reviews

11.1 Customers can rate and review completed Bookings. Reviews are checked and may be moderated by Staff before they are public. Approved reviews feed ratings and search.

11.2 You may reply once to a published review. You may not edit, hide, delete, trade, buy, incentivise or write reviews, ask Customers to withdraw a review, or retaliate. You may report a review for moderation.

11.3 Replies must be civil, factual and must not disclose health information or other personal data about the Customer.
