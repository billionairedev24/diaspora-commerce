# Privacy Notice

## Who this notice covers

This notice explains what personal information [[LEGAL: Q01 legal entity name]] ("AfroTransact", "we", "us") collects about you when you visit our websites, use our apps, shop with us, book a service, receive a delivery, talk to Lola, or contact us, and what we do with it. It also covers people who receive a gift card or a package ordered by someone else, and people who use a friend's referral link.

We are the business that decides how this information is used (a "controller", or a "business" under US state privacy laws). When we process information for a seller or service provider to deliver your order, they have their own responsibilities for what we give them. Payment processors act on their own terms for some data; see "Who we share it with".

## How we use it

- **To give you what you ask for:** create your account, process orders, bookings and payments, calculate and collect sales tax, deliver packages (including to a pickup point or on a delivery run), handle cancellations, help requests and refunds, and run store credit, gift cards, Plus membership and referrals.
- **To keep the Services safe:** prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, referral manipulation, chargebacks and unsafe listings; verify purchases for reviews; secure accounts.
- **To communicate with you:** send order, delivery, security and account messages by email, push, SMS and WhatsApp; answer support requests; and, only with your consent, send marketing.
- **To improve and personalise:** search, recommendations, Lola, and de-identified analytics on how the Services perform.
- **To advertise and measure advertising,** only where you have allowed advertising cookies or SDKs, and subject to your opt-out rights (see Your Privacy Choices).
- **To meet legal duties:** tax, accounting, sanctions and records, and to respond to lawful requests.

We rely on these legal bases where the law asks for one: performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests (security, fraud, service improvement), your consent (marketing messages, advertising cookies), and legal obligation.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only as described here.

- **Sellers and providers** receive what they need to fulfil your order or booking, such as your name, delivery address or pickup point, items ordered, and messages you send them. For deliveries we handle, the seller may get only the delivery status.
- **Delivery partners:** AfroTransact drivers, pickup-point operators and carriers (for example UPS, USPS, FedEx and DHL Express) receive the name, address, phone number and delivery instructions needed to deliver, and delivery events come back to us.
- **Payment and fraud services:** Stripe, Inc. processes your payments, saved payment methods and Link. Stripe collects payment, device and fraud-signal data and uses it under its own privacy policy as well as on our behalf. [[LEGAL: Q29 confirm whether Stripe is a controller, processor or independent business for each use, and how to describe it]]
- **Messaging services:** Twilio (SMS, with Amazon Web Services as a backup route), Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (WhatsApp), Resend and Amazon SES (email), and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service (push). They deliver the messages we ask them to and keep delivery records.
- **Address, map and routing services:** Google Maps Platform (address suggestions, location lookup and drive-time routing), Smarty (US address checks) and OpenStreetMap-based services. They receive what you type into an address field, or your coordinates, and the answer comes back to us.
- **AI services:** Lola and other AI features send your text, with personal details removed first where we can, to an AI gateway we run, which uses model providers reached through OpenRouter (for example Google and OpenAI models). See "AI features".
- **Hosting and infrastructure:** cloud, database, storage and content-delivery services, including Cloudflare, Layerbase and our cloud hosting providers, and monitoring and error-tracking services such as Grafana Cloud and Sentry. They store and process data for us under contracts that limit what they can do with it.
- **Advertising and analytics providers,** such as Google Ads and Meta, only if you allow advertising cookies. See the Cookie Notice for the exact list.
- **Professional advisers, auditors and authorities** where the law requires or to protect rights, safety and property, and **a buyer or successor** if the business is sold or reorganised (we will tell you and honour this notice).
- **People you choose:** for example the recipient of a gift card you send, or a friend who joined with your referral code (they see only that a referral was made, never your orders).

We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising features may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law; we only enable them for people who have not opted out, and you can opt out at any time through Your Privacy Choices or a Global Privacy Control signal. ## Mobile numbers, SMS and WhatsApp

We use your mobile number to send the order, delivery, booking, payment and security messages you asked for (including one-time passcodes) and, only if you separately opted in, marketing messages. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are excluded from every sharing category in this notice and will not be shared with any third party except the service providers that deliver the messages for us, who may not use it for their own marketing. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. WhatsApp messages need their own opt-in and are covered by the SMS and Notification Terms and WhatsApp's own terms.

## AI features

Lola, our AI shopping assistant, and some other features (for example search, translation, listing help and image checks) use AI models.

- Before a message goes to a model, we remove or mask things like email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, government ID numbers and street addresses where we can detect them. Lola's stored memory of a conversation is kept in the masked form.
- We route requests to model providers through OpenRouter. We configure prompt and response logging and training off where the provider allows it, and we do not allow model providers to use your content to train their models under our agreements. Model providers may keep data for a short time to run their service or for safety. [[LEGAL: Q16 verify these statements against OpenRouter's current terms and each routed model provider's data policy before launch]]
- Lola acts only on your account when you ask, and asks you to confirm important actions.
- AI output can be wrong. We do not use Lola or other AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects without human review.
- Do not put sensitive information such as health details, government ID numbers or full card numbers into a Lola chat.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, local storage, pixels and software development kits (SDKs). The Cookie Notice explains each category and lets you choose. We honour the Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of advertising cookies and of sale and sharing.

## Reviews, public content and referrals

Reviews show your first name and last initial and a "Verified purchase" label. Photos you add to a review are public. Messages with a seller are visible to that seller and to us. Referral programs use technical signals such as a sign-up device, network, delivery address and payment-card fingerprint (in a one-way form) to detect self-referral and abuse; they are used only for that purpose.

## How long we keep it

We keep order, payment, tax and credit-ledger records for 7 years for tax, accounting and legal reasons. We keep other information only as long as needed for the reason we collected it: for example, messages with support for as long as the matter is open and for a limited time afterwards, and marketing and consent records for as long as the consent is relied upon and the period afterwards in which we may need to prove it. When you close your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within 90 days, except what the law requires us to keep, what we need to handle open orders, disputes, fraud and unpaid balances, and consent and opt-out records. ## Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with a comprehensive privacy law), you may have the right to:

- know and access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct it, delete it, or get a portable copy;
- opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and of profiling that produces significant effects;
- limit the use of sensitive personal information (we use it only for the purposes the law allows);
- appeal a decision on your request; and
- not be treated worse for exercising these rights.

The page Your Privacy Choices explains how to make a request, how we verify it, how an authorised agent can act for you, and how to appeal. You can also email {privacy}. Residents of California can ask once a year which categories of personal information we shared with third parties for their direct marketing; we do not share it for that purpose. California residents under 18 who are registered users can ask us to remove content they posted.

## Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based and logged access, and separate storage for identity documents. Payment card data is handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.
