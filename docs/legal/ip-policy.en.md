# Intellectual Property and Copyright Policy

## Our approach

We respect the rights of creators, brands and owners and expect sellers, providers and customers to do the same. We do not allow counterfeit goods or content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights. The Prohibited and Restricted Items policy lists counterfeit goods among the things that are never allowed.

This policy is for reporting. It is not legal advice, and you should talk to a lawyer if you are unsure whether your rights are infringed.

## Reporting copyright infringement (DMCA notice)

If you believe that content on AfroTransact infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent. Under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. section 512), your notice must include:

1. Your physical or electronic **signature** (or that of a person authorised to act for the owner).
2. A description of the **copyrighted work** that you say is infringed (or, for many works on one site, a representative list).
3. A description of the **material you say infringes**, and where it is on AfroTransact, with enough detail for us to find it (for example, the web address of the listing).
4. Your **name, address, telephone number and email address.**
5. A statement that you have a **good-faith belief** that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
6. A statement that **the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner** of the right allegedly infringed.

**Be careful:** under section 512(f), a person who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, can be liable for damages. Please consider fair use and other exceptions before you send a notice.

## What we do when we receive a notice

If a notice is complete, we act promptly. We may remove or disable access to the material and tell the person who posted it (the seller, provider or customer) that we did so, and we may send them a copy of your notice, including your name and contact details. We may also take action on the account under the repeat infringer rules. We may share your notice with the person who posted the content and publish it in a database of takedown notices.

## Counter-notice (if your content was removed)

If you believe that your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to the designated agent above. It must include:

1. Your physical or electronic **signature.**
2. Identification of the material that was removed and **where it appeared** before it was removed.
3. A statement, **under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief** that the material was removed because of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your **name, address and telephone number,** and a statement that you **consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court** for the district where your address is (or, if you are outside the United States, any district where we can be found), and that you **will accept service of process** from the person who sent the notice or their agent.

When we receive a valid counter-notice, we send a copy to the person who sent the original notice and tell them that we will restore the material in **10 business days** unless they tell us within that time that they have filed a court action against you. If they do not, we may restore the material **no sooner than 10 and no later than 14 business days** after we receive the counter-notice. ## Repeat infringers

We will end the accounts of users and sellers who are repeat infringers, in appropriate circumstances. We treat an account as a repeat infringer when it has received [[LEGAL: Q26 repeat-infringer threshold, for example three valid notices not successfully counter-noticed within 12 months]]. We may also suspend or limit an account, hold payouts as our agreements allow, and remove listings on a first valid report if the infringement is clear or serious (for example counterfeit goods). We apply this policy fairly and consistently, and we may change the thresholds.

## Reporting trademark infringement and counterfeits

If you own a trademark and believe a listing or other content is counterfeit or uses your mark in a way that is likely to confuse customers, send a report to {legal} or use **Report** on the listing. Please include:

- Your name, company and contact details, and your relationship to the brand (owner or authorised agent);
- The trademark, with the registration number and registry (for example, the USPTO), and proof of rights if we ask;
- The link or listing ID and a description of why the item is counterfeit or infringing, with photos or other evidence where you can;
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised and that your report is accurate.

We review trademark and counterfeit reports promptly, and may remove the listing, cancel orders, refund buyers in full, suspend the seller, and share information with the rights owner and law enforcement where the law allows. We may ask the seller to respond or give proof (such as invoices or an authorisation to sell). Genuine resale of authentic goods ("first sale") and fair use of a brand name to describe a product can be lawful, so we may ask the seller for proof before we decide.

We do not decide trademark ownership. If both sides disagree, we may restore a listing if the reporter doesn't take legal action within a reasonable time. [[LEGAL: Q26 decide the trademark dispute process, the time limit and whether to offer a brand registry]]

## Other intellectual property

For reports about other rights (for example design rights, patents, trade secrets, or the right of publicity such as someone's image or voice), write to {legal} with the details. We may need a court order or proof.

## If you bought a counterfeit item

If you think an item you bought is counterfeit, report it through Your account > Get help with an order (see the Returns, Refunds and Problems With Your Order page). Our team will decide the outcome and a refund is made to the payers as described there. Please keep the item and its packaging.
