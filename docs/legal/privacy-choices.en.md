# Your Privacy Choices

## Your rights

This page explains the choices you have over your personal information and how to use them. It supports the Privacy Notice and the Cookie Notice. We offer these choices to everyone in the United States, whether or not your state law requires it. [[LEGAL: Q11 confirm whether to offer the rights to all US customers or only to residents of states with a law, and the applicable thresholds]]

Laws that give these rights include the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), the Colorado Privacy Act (CPA), the Connecticut Data Privacy Act (CTDPA), the Utah Consumer Privacy Act, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act and similar laws in other states. [[LEGAL: Q11 list of states in force at launch and in the launch markets]]

Depending on your state, you can:

- **Know and access.** Ask what personal information we collected about you, where it came from, why we use it, who we share it with, and get a copy of it.
- **Correct.** Ask us to fix inaccurate information.
- **Delete.** Ask us to delete the personal information we collected from you.
- **Port.** Get a copy of your data in a portable, usable format.
- **Opt out of sale and sharing.** Tell us not to sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
- **Opt out of targeted advertising and profiling.** Tell us not to use your information for targeted advertising, or for profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
- **Limit sensitive information.** Ask us to limit the use of sensitive personal information to what is needed to give you the Services.
- **Appeal** a decision on your request.
- **No retaliation.** We will not charge you different prices, deny you service or give you worse service for using these rights. Offering a program that depends on your data (for example Plus membership) is not retaliation, but we will tell you if deleting data means we cannot provide a benefit.

## Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising and measurement features may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some state laws. To opt out, you can:

1. Use the **Do not sell or share my personal information** link in the footer (or the toggle in Cookie preferences), or Account > Privacy in the app.
2. Turn on **Global Privacy Control** in your browser or a browser extension. We treat GPC as a valid opt-out request for that browser, and we also apply it to your account when you are signed in.
3. Email {privacy}.

Opting out turns off advertising cookies and stops us sending your information to advertising providers for those purposes. It does not stop the cookies and sharing needed to run the Services (for example fraud prevention at checkout, delivery to the seller and driver, and email delivery).

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. [[LEGAL: Q10 and Q11 confirm]]

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we honour it without asking you to take any further step. We apply it to the browser you are using, and to your account if you are signed in. We do not need you to confirm it. If GPC conflicts with a setting you have saved in your account, we apply the more privacy-protective option and tell you how to change it.

## Marketing choices

You can stop marketing messages at any time:

- **Email:** use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. It works in one click.
- **SMS:** reply STOP to any text. WhatsApp marketing has its own opt-in and opt-out; you can reply STOP or change it in Account > Notifications.
- **Push:** change the setting on your device or in Account > Notifications.

We will still send messages about your orders, deliveries, bookings, payments and security while your account is open. Details are in the SMS and Notification Terms.

## How we verify you

For requests to know, access, correct, delete or port data, we need to be sure that the request is from you or from someone you authorised. We send a confirmation link to the email address on your account. For deletion we also ask you to sign in. For sensitive or high-risk requests we may ask for a few more details that match what we already hold. We will not ask for more information than we need and we will use it only to check your identity. We do not need to verify opt-outs.

## Authorised agents

You can ask someone else to make a request for you. They must give us your signed permission (or a power of attorney), and we may check with you directly that you gave it. Opt-out requests sent by an agent through a GPC browser extension are treated as yours.

## How and when we respond

We confirm that we received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days of receiving it. If we need more time, we may extend that once by another 45 days and will tell you why. We respond by email or in your account. We will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly excessive or repeated; then we will tell you before we charge or refuse. [[LEGAL: Q11 confirm response times for each state, including 15 business days for opt-outs under CCPA and the number of free requests a year]]

If we cannot do what you ask, for example because the law requires us to keep a record, we will say so and explain why. Deleting your data means closing your account. We keep the records we are required to keep, such as tax and accounting records for 7 years, and records of your requests and opt-outs so that we can honour them.

Deleting your account also ends your store credit, membership benefits and referral rewards. If you have a store credit balance, use it first; credit cannot be converted to cash. See the Store Credit Terms.

## Appeals

If we decline or only partly complete your request, you can appeal. Reply to our response with the word "Appeal", or email {privacy} with the subject "Privacy appeal". A person who was not involved in the first decision will review it, and we will answer in writing within 60 days and explain the reasons. If the appeal is denied, you can raise a complaint with your state attorney general, and we will give you the contact details.
