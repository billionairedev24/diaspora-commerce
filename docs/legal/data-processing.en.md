"Order Data" is the part of Customer Data needed to fulfil an order or booking. "Sub-processor" is as defined on the Sub-processors list.

## 2. Roles of the parties

2.1 Independent controllers for fulfilment data. For Order Data (such as name, delivery address, phone number, email address if shared, order contents, delivery preferences, appointment details and service notes), you are an independent controller (or a "business" under U.S. law) for the purposes of fulfilling the order or booking, handling returns, claims and support for it, meeting your legal, tax and accounting duties, and defending legal claims. AfroTransact is a separate and independent controller for the data it collects to operate the Marketplace. Neither of us is the other's processor for Order Data.

2.2 AfroTransact as processor. Where AfroTransact processes personal data on your behalf for your own purposes (for example personal data of your team members that you add to Seller Center or Provider Hub, or data you ask us to store in your catalogue or notes), AfroTransact acts as your processor (or "service provider") and sections 4 to 12 apply to that processing. The subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data types and data subjects are described in Annex A.

2.3 AfroTransact as controller of your data. AfroTransact also processes data about you and your team (for onboarding, verification, risk, payments, support and legal compliance) as a controller, as described in the Privacy Notice.

2.4 Each party complies with Applicable Data Protection Law for its own processing. ## 3. What you may do with Customer Data

3.1 You must use Customer Data only to: fulfil the order or booking; communicate with the customer about it through the Marketplace; provide customer support and warranty service; comply with law; prevent fraud; and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

3.2 You must not: sell or share Customer Data (as those terms are defined by Applicable Data Protection Law), including for cross-context behavioural advertising; use it for your own marketing, promotions, newsletters or lists, or add the customer to any list, unless the customer has given you a separate lawful opt-in outside the order; combine it with data from other sources to profile the customer; contact the customer by text, WhatsApp, phone or email outside the Marketplace except as needed to fulfil the order, and never for marketing without valid consent; transfer it to anyone other than your own carriers, couriers and service providers who need it to fulfil the order and who are bound by confidentiality; or attempt to re-identify de-identified data.

3.3 You are responsible for your team members, couriers, carriers and service providers who receive Customer Data, and for their compliance.

3.4 You must give customers any notice that the law requires of you as a controller (for example a privacy notice available on request) and honour their rights as set out in section 8.

3.5 Sensitive data. If your Services involve sensitive personal data (for example health, religion, sex life, biometric or precise location data), you must comply with the extra legal requirements for it, obtain any consent required, and limit collection to what is necessary. ## 4. Security

4.1 Each party must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing, loss, destruction, alteration and disclosure, proportionate to the risk. At a minimum you must: restrict access to people who need it, with unique accounts and multi-factor authentication where offered; keep devices and software up to date; use encryption for data in transit and for devices that store Customer Data; keep Customer Data out of public spreadsheets, shared inboxes and personal accounts; train staff on privacy and security; protect printed shipping labels and packing slips; and securely dispose of data.

4.2 AfroTransact maintains the measures described in Annex B for the data it processes. We may update them provided the level of protection is not materially reduced.

4.3 Handover codes. You must keep customers' handover codes and delivery proof confidential and use them only for the delivery.

4.4 You must not copy Customer Data from Seller Center or Provider Hub into other systems except as needed to fulfil orders, and you must protect any copy.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each person you authorise to access Customer Data must be under a duty of confidentiality. Access must end when they leave or change role.

6.2 If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal data that we process on your behalf, we will notify you without undue delay and, where feasible, within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware [[LEGAL: confirm notice period]]. 6.5 Each controller is responsible for its own regulatory notifications. You bear the cost of incidents in your systems or caused by your people.

## Annex B. AfroTransact security measures (summary)

The following describes controls at the time of drafting, derived from the platform's documentation. Operations must confirm each before publication [[LEGAL: operations to verify each statement]].
- Access control: role-based access, with separation of duties for seller review (two different reviewers) and masked display of sensitive fields; reviewer views of documents and unmasked identifiers are logged.
- Authentication: central identity service, multi-factor options, step-up for sensitive administrative actions.
- Encryption: encryption in transit; sensitive identifiers (such as tax identification numbers) stored as masked values with field-level encryption; keys managed separately and rotated.
- Payment data: card and bank details are collected by Stripe and not stored by AfroTransact.
- Documents: verification documents are held in a restricted bucket and accessed through signed, logged links.
- Audit: an append-only, hash-chained audit log of staff decisions.
- Network and application security: vulnerability scanning, secret scanning, least-privilege service credentials, secrets held in a cloud secret store.
- Monitoring and incident response: metrics, logs and alerts, with an on-call process.
- Resilience: backups and restore procedures [[LEGAL: operations to describe backup frequency and testing]].
- Data minimisation: telemetry is scrubbed of personal data where practicable; handover codes are not stored in clear text in delivery event logs.
