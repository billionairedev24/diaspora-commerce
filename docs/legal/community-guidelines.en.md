# Community Guidelines

## Why these rules exist

Reviews, photos and messages help people buy with confidence and help sellers improve. They only work if they are honest and respectful. These guidelines are part of the Conditions of Use. Breaking them can lead to content being removed and, for serious or repeated cases, to limits on or closure of an account.

## Reviews and ratings

### Who can review

- **Verified purchase only.** You can review a product only if you received it: you need a delivered order for that product. Your review shows a "Verified purchase" label. Bookings can be reviewed after the service by the person who booked it.
- **One review per product.** You can post one review per product. You can edit your review for 30 days after you post it. After that it is final. You can delete your review at any time.
- If we cannot check your purchase, for example because of a system problem, we will ask you to try again, and we will not guess.

### What a good review is

Say what you bought, what you liked and what you did not, and how it was. You can add a title, text of up to 4,000 characters and up to 5 photos. Photos must be yours, of the item you received. Your review shows your first name and the first letter of your last name (for example "Adaeze N."). Nothing else about you is shown.

### What is not allowed

- **Fake or misleading reviews:** reviews by someone who did not buy or use the item, reviews written by or for the seller, their staff, family or competitors, reviews bought or traded, or reviews written by an AI tool and presented as your own experience.
- **Incentivised reviews:** reviews in exchange for money, free goods, credit, discounts, gifts or other rewards, or any promise of a better rating. Sellers must not offer anything for a review or ask for only positive ones, and must not ask a customer to change or remove a review. If a seller gave you a free product or other reward, do not review it here. (Referral and other AfroTransact program rewards are for referring friends, never for reviews.)
- **Review gating or suppression:** asking only happy customers to review, or threats and pressure to remove a review.
- Personal information of anyone (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses), links, contact details or ads.
- Hate speech, harassment, threats, sexual content, graphic violence, discrimination or abuse of any person or group.
- Content that infringes someone else's rights, or that breaks the law, or that is about a delivery or customer service experience that has nothing to do with the product (these are better reported to support).
- Spam, repeated text, off-topic content, and attempts to manipulate ratings.

You can say that a product was poor. You can't make false statements of fact about a seller or provider.

### How we check

We check every review by machine for things like bad language, links, email addresses and phone numbers. A review with none of these flags is published straight away. A flagged review is held and a member of our team reviews it, and may approve it, reject it with a reason, or remove it. If a review is rejected, you are told why and can edit it and send it again. Reasons include abusive content, personal information, spam, off-topic content, conflict of interest and other reasons. We may also remove a review later if we learn it breaks these guidelines. We do not remove a review because it is negative, and we do not remove reviews at the request of a seller unless it breaks these rules.

### Reporting a review

Use **Report** on any review to tell us why it breaks these guidelines. We review reports and take action. Reports are looked at by our team; a report is not a guarantee that a review will be removed.

### Seller replies

The seller of a product can post a public reply to a review. Replies must follow the same rules: be polite, factual and about the product or the experience, and not include personal information of the reviewer or offers of a reward for changing the review. A seller may report a review they think breaks these guidelines. Sellers cannot edit or delete your review.

## Messages with sellers, providers, drivers and support

Messages on AfroTransact are for your orders, bookings and deliveries. Please be respectful and honest. You may not:

- Harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against anyone.
- Ask for or send personal, payment or banking details outside the way our checkout works, or ask someone to pay or deal off AfroTransact to avoid fees or protections.
- Send spam, ads, scams, malware or links that are not about the order.
- Share the delivery code with anyone other than the person handing over your package.
- Pretend to be someone else, including AfroTransact staff.
- Share another person's private information or photos without their permission.
- Send sexual content, or content that is illegal.

We can see messages sent through AfroTransact and may review them to resolve problems, prevent fraud and enforce these rules. See the Privacy Notice. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services first and then report to us.

Sellers, providers and drivers follow rules at least as strict as these in their own agreements.

## Using Lola, our AI shopping assistant

Lola is an AI assistant. It can help you find products, track a package, change delivery instructions and get help with an order, and it will ask you to confirm important actions.

- **Lola is a machine and can be wrong.** Check important details, such as price, delivery date, ingredients and allergens, on the product page or label. Do not rely on Lola for medical, legal, tax, financial or immigration advice.
- **Do not put sensitive information in a chat:** health details, government ID numbers, full card numbers or bank details, or other people's private details. Lola tries to mask personal details before it sends text to a model, but you should not rely on that.
- **Use Lola fairly.** Don't use it to try to break our systems, to get others' account information, to make prohibited items, to harass or to produce harmful content, to extract Lola's instructions, or to run automated or high-volume requests. Don't use Lola to write fake reviews.
- Lola can hand over to a person if needed, with a short, masked summary.
- Lola's answers are not an offer or a promise by AfroTransact. A price or policy that Lola states does not override the product page or our written terms. You can ask Lola to start a request to a person at any time.
- Lola chats are handled under the Privacy Notice. AI features rely on model providers; please see that notice.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, use **Report** on the page or email {legal}. We may remove content, warn you, limit what you can post, cancel an order, withhold rewards, or suspend or close an account, depending on how serious or repeated the breach is. We try to be fair and tell you why where we can. You can ask us to review a decision by replying to our message or through Account > Help. Reports about intellectual property go through the Intellectual Property and Copyright Policy.
