# Gift Card Terms

## What a gift card is

An AfroTransact gift card is a digital card with a value in US dollars (USD) that you can send to someone by email. When the recipient redeems it, the value is added to their AfroTransact store credit balance, and they can spend it on products and services on AfroTransact. These terms apply to the gift card and, once redeemed, also to the credit it becomes (see the Store Credit Terms).

Gift cards are issued by [[LEGAL: Q01 legal entity name]] ("AfroTransact", "we"). They are not issued by a bank, and they are not a deposit account, a debit card or a prepaid card. [[LEGAL: Q08 and Q12 confirm the legal characterisation of a closed-loop digital gift card under federal and state law, including the CARD Act and state gift-certificate laws]]

## Buying a gift card

- You can buy a gift card for any amount from the minimum to the maximum shown on the gift card page. At launch, that is **$10 to $500** per card. These limits are set by AfroTransact and can change; the page always shows the current limits. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm limits, per-day and per-customer purchase limits and any state limits]]
- You pay with a card or another payment method shown at checkout, and you agree to the Conditions of Use and Stripe's terms for that payment. The payment is collected when you buy, because nothing is shipped. You cannot pay for a gift card with store credit or another gift card. [[LEGAL: Q12 confirm]]
- You enter the recipient's email address and a message, and choose to send it now or on a date you pick. We send the card by email on that date. You are responsible for entering the right address. Please check it, because we cannot know who owns an address.
- A gift card may be refused or cancelled, and the amount refunded, if we think that the purchase may be fraud or a payment is reversed.
- A gift card is paid for in full at purchase, so it is not boosted by Plus membership or referral offers. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm which membership and promotional benefits apply to gift card purchases]]

## Getting the card

The email contains a code. The code is secret: whoever has it can redeem the card. We store the code in a form that we cannot read back, so we cannot look it up for you from the code alone.

## Redeeming a gift card

1. Sign in to your AfroTransact account (or create one).
2. Go to Your account > AfroTransact credit and enter the code, or follow the button in the email.
3. The value is added to your store credit balance at once.

A card can be redeemed once only. After redemption, the balance is **store credit** in the redeemer's account, and it follows the Store Credit Terms. A gift card cannot be split, merged or moved to someone else after it is redeemed. For safety, we limit how many times a code can be tried in a short time, and we may ask you to try later or to verify your identity.

## No expiry, no fees

Our gift cards do not expire, and we do not charge dormancy, service or inactivity fees, unless the law allows or requires otherwise. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm there is no state in the launch footprint that requires a different rule; if a fee or expiry is ever introduced it must follow the applicable state law and CARD Act rules]]

## No cash

A gift card and the credit it becomes cannot be exchanged for cash, paid out, refunded to a card, or transferred to a bank account, except where a law requires us to give cash back. [[LEGAL: Q08 some states, for example California, require cash back for small remaining balances (often under $10); counsel to list the states and the rule]]

## Cancelling and refunds

- **Before the card is sent or used,** you can ask us to cancel a gift card you scheduled for a future date: open a help request (Your account > Get help with an order) with the order details. Our team cancels (voids) a card that has not been redeemed and refunds the amount you paid to the card or payment method you used. There is no cancel button yet, so this is done by our team. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm the wording]]
- **After the card is sent,** it cannot be cancelled and the purchase is not refundable, except where the law requires it, or where we decide in a help request that the card was bought in error or by fraud and has not been redeemed.
- **If the card was bought with a payment that is later disputed or reversed,** we may remove the unspent balance and recover the amount.

## Lost, stolen or wrong email

Treat a gift card code like cash. We are not responsible for a card that is lost, stolen, sent to the wrong address or used by someone else after you share the code. If you think that a code was sent to the wrong person, or was stolen, tell us at once through Your account > Get help with an order or {phone}. If the card has not been redeemed, we may be able to cancel it and issue a new one after we check your identity and the purchase, which we do at our discretion. If it has been redeemed, the credit is in someone's account and we cannot take it back unless it was obtained by fraud.

## Fraud and scams

AfroTransact will never ask you to pay by gift card, to read out a gift card code over the phone or chat, or to buy gift cards for an agency or a prize. If someone asks you to, it is a scam. Tell us. We may freeze or cancel a card, or the credit it became, that we believe is linked to fraud, a scam, money laundering, sanctions, a stolen payment method or a breach of our terms, and we may report it to the authorities.

## Using gift card credit

Credit from a gift card can be used for products and services on AfroTransact, subject to the Store Credit Terms. It can pay for all or part of an order; the rest is paid by card. Refunds go back to whoever paid, the way they paid; credit paid is returned as credit. Credit cannot be used to buy gift cards. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm]]

## Unclaimed property

Some states have unclaimed property (escheat) laws that may apply to unused gift cards or credit. We follow the law that applies. We may need to hold or report details of unredeemed gift cards or unused balances, and we may contact you before we do. [[LEGAL: Q08 which states exempt gift cards from escheat, and what to say about balances that we might have to report]]

## Our right to change these terms

We can change these terms for gift cards bought in the future. A change will not reduce the value of a card you already hold. Gift cards are also covered by the Conditions of Use.
