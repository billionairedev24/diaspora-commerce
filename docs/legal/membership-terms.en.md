# Plus Membership Terms

## What this is

AfroTransact offers paid memberships with benefits for shoppers. We call the first one **Plus**. We may offer other tiers (for example a higher one), and we may change which tiers are offered. These terms apply to every tier. The plan name, price, trial length and benefits that apply to you are shown on the sign-up page and in the confirmation email, and in Account > Membership. If anything in this document is different from what the sign-up page shows for your tier, the sign-up page for your tier wins for price and benefits.

The membership is provided by [[LEGAL: Q01 legal entity name]] ("AfroTransact", "we").

## Example of the Plus tier at launch

The Plus tier at launch is planned to be $9.99 a month or $99 a year (USD), with a 14-day free trial, and free AfroTransact delivery and free carrier shipping on orders of $25 or more. These amounts are examples and can change before launch; the sign-up page shows the exact price, trial and benefits that apply when you join. ## Benefits

Each tier has a set of benefits that we choose from a list and that we may change over time. Benefits can include:

- **Free delivery and carrier shipping** on orders over an order minimum, up to a limit per order. Pickup and express options are not covered.
- **Credit back** on delivered orders, as a percent of what you spend, paid as store credit.
- **A monthly credit** paid as store credit.
- **Early access** to pre-order windows and delivery runs.
- **Priority support.**
- **Bigger referral or gift rewards.**
- **Member prices or deals,** where shown on a product.

The benefits of your tier are shown at sign-up and in Account > Membership, and each order shows the benefits that were applied. Credit benefits start with your first paid period and not during a free trial. Store credit from a membership follows the Store Credit Terms, so it can never be converted to cash. If a benefit is changed or removed, we will give you notice, and you can cancel. The changes do not affect benefits already earned on orders already placed.

## Free trial

If your tier has a trial, the sign-up page tells you how long it is and what you will pay when it ends. A trial is available once per customer and once per card. If you started a membership before, or you use a card that has already been used for a trial, you are not eligible, and you will be told that you will be charged for the first period when you join. **Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your membership continues and we will charge your payment method the price of the first period.** We will remind you before the trial ends (see "Reminders").

## Automatic renewal: what you agree to

**Please read this section.** When you join, you agree that:

1. Your membership **renews automatically** at the end of each period (each month for a monthly plan, each year for a yearly plan) **until you cancel**.
2. We will **charge the payment method you chose** (or the one you later set as your default for the membership) for the price shown **at the start of each period**, plus any tax that applies, using the card authorisation you give us at sign-up. This is a recurring charge. You authorise us and Stripe to make it without asking you again each time.
3. The price is the price shown to you at sign-up. We lock it for your membership. If we want to change the price for existing members, we will tell you in advance, by email, at least [[LEGAL: Q09 notice period for price changes; some state laws require a specific period, for example 7 to 30 days]] days before the change and before the next charge, and you can cancel before it takes effect.
4. You can **cancel at any time** (see "How to cancel"). We will not charge you again after you cancel.

You give your express consent to these terms by ticking the unticked consent box (or by the equivalent clear action) and pressing the button on the sign-up page, which shows the price, how often you will be charged, the length of the trial, that the membership renews until you cancel, and how to cancel, next to the button, before you pay. We send you a confirmation by email after you sign up, with these terms and instructions on how to cancel. We keep a record of what you agreed to, when, and which version of these terms.

## How to cancel

You can cancel **online, any time, in the same way you signed up**: go to Account > Membership and choose **Cancel membership**. You can also cancel from the confirmation email link, in the mobile app, or by contacting support at {phone} or through Account > Help. You do not have to call, chat with an agent, give a reason, or go through special offers to cancel. After you cancel, we show a confirmation on screen and by email.

When you cancel:

- No further renewal charge is made.
- Your benefits **continue until the end of the period you already paid for**, unless you take the refund described below, in which case they end at once.
- If you cancel during a trial, you will not be charged and the membership ends at the end of the trial, unless you ask for it to end at once.
- You can **resume** the membership at any time before the end of the period, and the cancellation is undone. After it ends, you can join again.

## Refunds

If you cancel **within [[LEGAL: Q09 refund window, currently 7 days by platform default]] days after a charge, and before you have used any benefit** of that period (for example before a free delivery waiver was applied to an order, or before credit was paid), you can ask for the whole membership fee back to the card you paid with, and your membership ends at once. The membership page shows whether you qualify and why not if you do not (for example, the window has passed or a benefit was used). Otherwise, the fee is not refundable and your benefits continue to the end of the period. Our team can refund a fee in other cases with a reason, such as when we charged you by mistake. Store credit already paid to you as a membership benefit is not paid out in cash; if you take a refund, the credit-back from that period may be taken back.

Where the law gives you a right to a refund, that right applies.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, your membership shows as past due. We try again on the days we set (by default, after 1, 3 and 5 days). Your benefits stay on while we retry. We will email you each time and ask you to update your payment method. If the last try fails, the membership ends (lapses) and the benefits stop. You can join again at any time. You are not charged for a period where the payment failed.

## Changing your plan

- **Upgrading** to a higher tier or a longer interval takes effect straight away. We charge the difference for the unused part of the current period.
- **Downgrading,** or changing from yearly to monthly, takes effect at the end of the current period.
- You can take back a scheduled change before it takes effect.

## Reminders

We send you these notices by email and in the app (and by SMS or push if you chose those):

- A confirmation when you sign up, with the price, trial, renewal terms and how to cancel.
- A reminder before a trial ends and before the first charge. [[LEGAL: Q09 timing, for example 3 to 7 days before]]
- A reminder before a **yearly** renewal [[LEGAL: Q09 timing required by state law, for example 15 to 45 days before]], and a notice of a price change or a material change in terms.
- A receipt after each charge.

## Complimentary membership

Sometimes we give free days of membership, for example as a referral reward or in a promotion. A free membership has an end date and does not need a card. It ends on that date and does not renew into a paid membership unless you choose to join. If you are already a member, free days are added to your current period.

## Taxes

Membership fees are shown before tax. We add sales tax where the law requires it. [[LEGAL: Q20 confirm whether membership fees are taxable in the launch states]]

## Using your membership fairly

Membership is for personal and household use. We can end a membership or take back benefits if we reasonably find abuse, such as repeated returns of a delivery benefit, creating accounts or cards to get more free trials, or reselling goods. We will tell you why, and we will refund the unused part of the current period if we end a membership for a reason other than your breach. See the Conditions of Use.

## Payments by Stripe

Membership fees are processed by Stripe on a card or bank account you saved. By saving the card you agree to Stripe's terms and acknowledge its privacy policy. You can change the payment method for your membership in Account > Payments.

## Changes to these terms

We will tell you at least 30 days before a material change to these terms or to your tier, and you can cancel before it takes effect. A change never reduces a period that you have already paid for.
