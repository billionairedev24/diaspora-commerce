# Referral Program Terms

## How the program works

The referral program is run and paid for by AfroTransact ([[LEGAL: Q01 legal entity name]], "we"). It is not run by sellers or providers. When you share your referral code or link and a friend signs up with it and then makes a qualifying order, we reward you both.

The amounts, rules and dates below are examples. We set the real ones in the program settings, and the app shows the current reward for you and for your friend, and the conditions, before you share and before your friend signs up. If something here is different from what the app shows, the app shows what applies.

## Example of the rewards

At launch we plan this reward: **$10 in store credit for you and $10 in store credit for your friend,** after your friend's **first order of at least $30 has been delivered and its problem-reporting period has passed** (the "return window", which is **30 days** after delivery by default). A reward can instead be a number of days of free membership, or a different amount, and it can be different for the person who refers and the person who is referred. The program may also have limits (see "Limits").

## Who can take part

- You must have an AfroTransact account in good standing and be 18 or older.
- Your friend must be a **new customer** who has never had an AfroTransact account, and who signs up using your code or link. A code typed after sign-up cannot be added later. [[LEGAL: Q24 confirm]]
- You cannot refer yourself, someone in your household, or an account you control. Staff accounts and business accounts are not eligible. [[LEGAL: Q24 confirm]]

## When a reward is earned

A reward is earned only when **all** the following are true:

1. Your friend signed up with your code or link, within the time limit the app shows (by default 90 days from sign-up to qualify).
2. Your friend placed an order that meets the minimum order value, and that order was **delivered**. Only the value of goods that stayed delivered counts. If the order is refunded so that it falls below the minimum, it does not qualify.
3. The problem-reporting (return) window for that order has passed without it being refunded below the minimum.
4. The program is on, you and your friend have passed our checks, and neither of you is breaking these terms.

Where a delivery or refund is still being reviewed, the reward waits. If a smaller first order does not reach the minimum, a later delivered order can still qualify within the time limit. You can see a referral that is waiting in Your account > Refer a friend.

## How rewards are paid

Rewards are paid as **store credit** (or, if the app says so, free days of membership). They follow the Store Credit Terms, so credit can only be used to buy products and services and can never be converted to cash, and cannot be transferred. A reward is not income paid for a service, and it is not for sale. [[LEGAL: Q24 confirm tax treatment, and whether reporting is needed for high-value referrers]] A reward paid to a Plus member may be larger if the member's tier gives bigger rewards.

## Limits

We may limit how many rewarded referrals one person can have and how much referral credit they can earn in total. By default, the limits are 20 rewarded referrals and $500 of referral credit per person. The limits apply to each side on its own, so your limit does not stop your friend from being rewarded. The app shows the current limits. [[LEGAL: Q24 confirm]]

## Abuse rules and checks

Do not abuse the program. You may not: create fake or duplicate accounts; refer yourself or people in your household; use a friend's payment method, address or device to pass the checks; post your code on coupon, deal or paid-advertising sites in a way that suggests that it is a public discount; send spam or unwanted messages; use misleading claims about AfroTransact or the reward; pay or trade for referrals; or place orders only to collect the reward and then cause them to be refunded.

To find abuse, we compare technical signals from sign-up and ordering: the sign-up device and network, the delivery address and the saved payment card (stored in a one-way form, not in the clear). The same device, network, address or card on both the referrer and the friend is a sign that two accounts belong to the **same household or person**. **We allow one referral reward per household.** [[LEGAL: Q24 and Q11 confirm the privacy wording and how to describe a household]] Referrals that look suspicious are held for review by our team. You will see a held referral as "waiting" and not the reason.

## Our rights

We may **withhold, reduce, delay or cancel** a reward, and take back credit already paid, if we reasonably believe the rules were broken, a payment was reversed or an order was refunded or cancelled, or the referral came from abuse. We may suspend or end an account involved in abuse. We decide if a referral qualifies; our decision will be explained if you ask. We can change, pause or end the program at any time; **changes apply to future referrals**, and a reward already earned is honoured. If the program is switched off, referrals pause and resume when it is back on, within the time limits.

## Your friend's privacy

When you refer a friend, you see only whether the referral is pending or rewarded; you never see their orders, name or address. Your friend sees that they joined with a code. Please only share your code with people who want to hear from you. Do not send messages that break the law, including the rules on commercial texts and email: your friend must have agreed to hear from you. We do not send marketing texts to your friend because of your referral unless they opt in to them.

## Promotional claims

If you tell others about AfroTransact online and you are rewarded, you must say clearly that you get a reward for referrals, as the FTC's endorsement rules require. [[LEGAL: Q24 confirm disclosure wording and whether program is a promotion subject to state registration]]
