# Seller Fees Summary

> DRAFT. This document was prepared with AI assistance and has not been reviewed by qualified counsel. It summarises the Seller Terms; if the two differ, the Seller Terms and Service Provider Terms control. Any number on this page is a labelled example, not a current rate. Text in double square brackets marked LEGAL is an open decision.

## 1. How to use this page

1.1 This page explains in plain language what you pay AfroTransact and how each charge is worked out. It does not list prices. AfroTransact Staff configure plans, commission rates, connection fees and delivery fees, and they can change them. The live numbers that apply to you are always in Seller Center (or Provider Hub) under Plan & billing and the rate card.

1.2 Where this page gives a number, it is marked "Example" and uses made-up figures to show the arithmetic.

## 2. The short version

You may pay up to five kinds of charge:

| Charge | Who pays it | When it is taken |
|---|---|---|
| Plan fee | Sellers and providers on a paid plan | On each billing cycle (monthly, quarterly or annual, as offered) |
| Commission | Sellers, on product sales | When the customer's payment for the goods is captured |
| Connection fee | Providers, on service bookings | When the Deposit, Balance or kept amount is captured |
| Delivery, label and handling fees | Sellers, when the fee rule or the label purchase assigns the cost to them | As set out in section 6 |
| Stripe fees | Your connected account and the payment allocated to your lines | As Stripe charges them |

AfroTransact Retail, our own first-party retail business, pays no commission or plan fee to AfroTransact.

## 3. Plan fee

3.1 A plan is a subscription with a price per billing cycle, features and limits (for example the number of listings you may have, bulk upload, an image library, coupons and AI tools). Staff create and retire plans. A monthly price of zero is a commission-only plan.

3.2 Plan versions. When Staff change a plan's price or trial, they publish a new version. Existing subscribers stay on the version they pay until their next renewal or until they accept a migration; a price change reaches you at your next renewal and you will have notice as set out in the Seller Terms.

3.3 Trials, free months and rewards. A plan can include a trial or free months, and Staff may run promotions and reward rules, for example a free period if sales in your first period are below a stated amount, or a bonus for reaching a sales or listing goal. Each has stated conditions, and decisions are made once, at the time stated. Seller Center shows the rule in plain words and what applies to you.

3.4 Charge conditions. A plan can be free until you reach a sales or listing threshold, free while you stay under a threshold, or free for some months and then charged. Seller Center shows the charge terms of your plan.

3.5 Negotiated plans. If AfroTransact agrees a different price or terms with you, it is applied as a private plan for your organisation.

## 4. How a plan fee is paid: credits, then earnings (if you opt in), then card

When a plan invoice is created, it is settled in this order:

1. Credits, rewards, coupons and free-period balances on your account are applied first.
2. If you have chosen "Pay your plan from your sales" (and this option is switched on), AfroTransact takes what it can from your eligible pending earnings: money from delivered orders that is not under a dispute and not yet transferred to you. Only up to the amount of the invoice is taken.
3. Anything that remains is charged to the card or bank account you chose for billing.

You can change the choice in Seller Center; a change applies from your next invoice. If the invoice is voided or credited, money taken from earnings is given back. The option can be switched off, in which case invoices go to your card. AfroTransact Retail is never billed.

## 5. Commission on product sales

5.1 Commission is a percentage of the value of the goods you sell, taken when the customer's payment for those goods is captured. It is not charged on orders that are cancelled before capture.

5.2 Which rate applies. Staff configure the rate. When an order is placed, the system picks the first match from this list:
1. Retail: AfroTransact Retail pays nothing.
2. A negotiated rate for your organisation (dated, for a type of fee, optionally for a category), if one is active.
3. The rate for your plan and the product's category.
4. The rate for the product's category.
5. The default rate.

Seller Center shows which source applied to each category (for example "Negotiated rate until [date]", "Your plan", "Category rate" or "Standard rate").

5.3 The rate is recorded on each order line when the order is placed. Later changes to plans, categories or negotiated rates do not change orders already placed.

5.4 Example (made-up numbers). Goods sold for $100.00 with a commission rate of 10 percent: commission is $10.00; your share of the goods before Stripe cost allocation is $90.00.

5.5 Commission is shown on your statements as its own column, separate from other fees.

## 6. Delivery, shipping, labels and handling

6.1 Delivery fees are rules, not code constants. Staff configure delivery fee rules by method: AfroTransact Delivery (by distance band), carrier shipping (rate, handling and a free-shipping threshold), and pickup (often no charge). The rule that applies is shown to the customer at checkout and recorded on the order.

6.2 Who bears the cost. Depending on the fulfilment method and the rule:
- Carrier labels bought through AfroTransact: the carrier's cost plus a handling charge are charged to you and deducted from your balance.
- Fulfilment by AfroTransact: a per-unit fulfilment fee set in the fee schedule (and possibly by plan or negotiated) applies, and AfroTransact's own shipping may be charged to the customer.
- AfroTransact Delivery from your own location: a seller-side delivery fee set by the fee rule may be charged to you.
- Seller-delivered and pickup orders: only any fee the rule provides.

6.3 Delivery runs. A delivery run batches orders for a place into one trip. Customers pay at checkout; if a run is cancelled, every order on it is refunded in full automatically.

6.4 Fault rule on refunds. When a refund involves a delivery that has already been made or attempted, the cost of the trip is allocated to whoever is at fault:
- If the problem is yours (a wrong, damaged or missing item, or a cancellation by you after pickup), the customer can get the delivery charge back and AfroTransact keeps the seller-side delivery fee.
- If the customer changed their mind or refused the delivery, the delivery charge is not refundable, and the seller-side fee is given back in proportion to the items refunded.
- Shipments cancelled before pickup cost nothing.

## 7. Connection fee for service providers

7.1 A connection fee is a percentage of the service money that AfroTransact actually captures for you, taken at the moment of capture. Staff configure it by plan, by service category and by negotiated rate, with the same first-match order as commission.

7.2 When it applies:

| Situation | Connection fee |
|---|---|
| Deposit captured at confirmation | On the deposit |
| Balance or full price captured at completion | On the balance |
| Late customer cancellation or no-show where you keep the deposit or no-show amount | On the amount you keep |
| Cancelled or declined before anything is captured | None |
| Refund | Returned in proportion if the fee schedule says so; otherwise kept by AfroTransact |

7.3 Provider late-cancellation fee. If you cancel late or do not attend, a separate provider fee may be charged to you. Staff configure its amount and timing, and it is shown in Provider Hub.

7.4 Provider Hub shows "what you keep" before you accept a request: price less the connection fee and estimated card fees. It is an estimate.

7.5 Example (made-up numbers). A $100.00 service with a $20.00 deposit and an 8 percent connection fee: fee on the deposit $1.60 when captured; on the $80.00 balance $6.40 when captured; total $8.00 before card fees.

## 8. Stripe and payout costs

8.1 Stripe charges processing fees on customer payments and account and payout fees under the Stripe agreements. As configured, your connected account bears Stripe's account and payout fees, and the processing cost of each payment is allocated across the lines in proportion to their value and shown on your statement. See the Payment Terms.

8.2 Example (made-up numbers). A $100.00 customer payment with a Stripe cost of $3.20 for the whole order is split across the lines; your lines carry your proportional share.

## 9. Taxes

9.1 Sales tax is calculated, collected and remitted by AfroTransact on eligible sales as described in the Seller Terms; it is not part of your commission base and is shown separately at checkout. Fees we charge you may themselves attract tax, which is added where it applies. [[LEGAL: confirm tax treatment and wording once tax registration status is known]]

## 10. How and when amounts reach you

Your net earnings are: the item price and your share of shipping, less commission or connection fee, less the allocated Stripe cost, less any label, plan-fee or other deductions. Net earnings for an order become eligible for transfer after the hold window that follows delivery (or service completion), then you can request a payout. See the Seller Terms and Payment Terms.
