# Accessibility Statement

## Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to shop, book and get help on AfroTransact, including people who use screen readers, magnification, voice control, switch devices, or only a keyboard. We aim for our websites and apps to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA**. We treat an accessibility problem as a defect to fix, not a special request.

This statement applies to the AfroTransact storefront, the account, cart, checkout and order pages, the help center, the seller and provider tools, and our mobile apps. [[LEGAL: Q17 confirm the scope of this statement, including the staff tools and the driver app]]

## What we support

- Using the whole site with a keyboard alone, with a "Skip to main content" link as the first stop on every page and a clearly visible focus outline that is never hidden behind a sticky header.
- Screen readers: labelled controls, headings in order, landmarks, descriptive names for icons and images, and announcements (live regions) for changes such as cart updates and errors. We test with VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA and keyboard-only navigation.
- Text resizing up to 200% and reflow to a narrow screen without losing content or function.
- Colour contrast that meets Level AA, checked automatically against our design tokens. Seller storefront themes must meet minimum contrast before they can be published.
- Reduced motion, and high-contrast and dark themes where offered.
- Touch targets of at least 44 pixels in our apps and on small screens.
- Captions on videos we publish, and text alternatives for images.
- Languages: the page language and reading direction follow your language, including right-to-left languages such as Arabic.
- Forms with clear labels, error summaries that tell you what to fix, and no time limits you cannot extend.

## How we check

Our automated tests run an accessibility engine (axe-core) against the key pages of each website on every change, and fail the build for serious or critical problems. We also run keyboard and preference journeys (skip links, focus, dialogs, reduced motion, right-to-left) and review design changes for accessibility. Automatic checks find only part of the problems, so we also test by hand with assistive technologies, and we plan for an independent audit. ## Known limitations

- Some product images uploaded by sellers have no good text description. We are adding AI-suggested descriptions that sellers review before they are published.
- Some third-party services we use, such as the secure payment fields from Stripe, the map and address suggestion tools, and embedded content from other companies, are built by others. We choose providers that work to accessibility standards and report problems to them, but we do not control their code.
- Documents and files uploaded by sellers and providers may not be accessible.

## If you need help or find a barrier

Tell us. Email {accessibility} or call {phone}. Please tell us the page or screen, what you were trying to do, what got in the way, and the device, browser or assistive technology you use. We reply within 2 business days, and we will try to give you the information or service you wanted by another route, for example by placing an order for you by phone, while we fix the problem. You do not need to explain your disability.

We keep a record of reports, fix them in priority order, and tell you when we have. [[LEGAL: Q17 remediation timeline commitments, and whether to name an accessibility coordinator]]
