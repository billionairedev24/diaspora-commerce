# Prohibited and Restricted Items

## Why we have this policy

We want you to buy with confidence. Some things cannot be sold on AfroTransact because they are illegal, unsafe, or break the rules of the payment, messaging and delivery companies we work with (for example Stripe, Twilio, WhatsApp and the parcel carriers). Some things can be sold only by approved sellers with a licence. This page is a summary. The seller and provider policy is more detailed, and we may update the lists as laws and partner rules change.

## Never allowed

These cannot be listed, sold, bought or sent through AfroTransact, whatever the seller says.

- Weapons, firearms, parts and ammunition, explosives and fireworks.
- Illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia, and substances such as kratom or products with unapproved psychoactive ingredients, wherever the law restricts them.
- Counterfeit, fake, replica or unauthorised copies of branded goods, and pirated media or software.
- Stolen goods, and goods or services that help someone commit a crime, fraud or evade taxes or sanctions.
- Wildlife and wildlife products that are protected, such as items covered by CITES, bushmeat, ivory and live animals.
- Human remains, body parts or bodily fluids.
- Recalled items, and items that are unsafe or banned for sale in the US.
- Medicines that need a prescription, and unapproved medicines, supplements and treatments that claim to cure or treat a disease, or that have been found to contain hidden drugs.
- Adult sexual content and services, gambling, lotteries, and pyramid or multi-level schemes.
- Personal information, identity documents, hacked accounts, or surveillance tools used for stalking.
- Gift cards and store credit sold on, or offered as a way to get around our rules, and virtual currency.
- Hate content and items that promote violence or harassment of a group of people.

## Restricted: allowed only with approval

These can be sold only by sellers and providers whom we have approved and who hold the licence or permit the law requires.

- Alcohol, tobacco, vapes and nicotine products, and hemp or CBD products, if offered at all in your state. Where allowed, they are age-checked at delivery. [[LEGAL: Q18 decide which of these are allowed at launch, by state]]
- Meat, poultry, seafood, dairy and other foods that need a licence or cold storage.
- Raw shea and herbal products marketed for health.
- Cosmetics and skin products, which must not contain mercury, hydroquinone above the allowed limit, or other banned ingredients.
- Medical devices and health products regulated by the FDA.
- Immigration, tax, legal or financial services. Providers must be licensed, accredited or authorised to give that advice where the law requires it, and must say so.
- Home, personal care and beauty services that need a state licence.

## Food, perishables and imported goods

Many of our customers shop for foods from home. These rules protect you.

- Imported foods must meet FDA rules, carry English-language labelling with ingredients, allergens and the importer, and come from registered facilities. Sellers must not list food that is unlabelled, expired, damaged or unsafe.
- Perishable and frozen items are sold only for delivery methods that can keep them safe, and we show that on the listing. Pre-order delivery windows and delivery runs may be used for perishable items; the Pre-order and Delivery Run Terms explain what to do on delivery. Do not eat food that arrives spoiled, damaged or with a broken seal. Report it through Account > Help.
- Home-made foods sold from a home kitchen must follow state cottage food laws, including limits on what can be sold and shipped.
- Allergen and ingredient information on a listing is provided by the seller. If you have an allergy, check the label on the product when it arrives.
- Items that cannot be shipped lawfully into a state or by a given carrier will not be offered to addresses where they are not allowed.

## Shipping limits

Some safe and legal items still cannot travel by every method, for example flammable liquids, aerosols, batteries, perfumes and other hazardous materials. Sellers must label and ship such goods properly or not list them. Items that carriers refuse will not be delivered by those carriers.

## Services

A service must be legal where it is performed. Providers must hold the required licence or insurance and keep them current. Services must not be a way to sell a prohibited item or to take a customer off AfroTransact to pay.

## What happens if a rule is broken

We may remove a listing or a message, cancel an order and refund you in full, suspend or close the seller's or provider's account, withhold payouts that the law or our agreements allow us to hold, tell a payment, carrier or messaging partner, and report to the authorities where required. If you try to buy something prohibited, we may cancel your order and limit your account.

## Reporting a listing

If you see a listing, review or message that should not be here, use **Report** on the page, or email {legal}. Include the link and what is wrong. We review reports within one business day. For urgent safety problems, such as a product that made you ill, contact us straight away at {phone} and also contact your doctor or local emergency services as needed. See also the Intellectual Property and Copyright Policy for counterfeit and copyright reports.
