# Store Credit Terms

## What store credit is

Store credit is a balance in US dollars (USD) on your AfroTransact account. You can use it to pay for products and services on AfroTransact. It is kept as a running record (a ledger) of every addition and every use, so your balance is always the total of that record, and you can see the record in Your account > AfroTransact credit.

Store credit is issued by [[LEGAL: Q01 legal entity name]] ("AfroTransact", "we"). It is not money in a bank account. It is not a deposit, does not earn interest, is not insured by the FDIC or any government agency, and is not a payment instrument or a prepaid card. [[LEGAL: Q12 confirm the characterisation and whether any state stored-value, money-transmitter or prepaid access rule applies to closed-loop credit]]

## How you can get store credit

- **Refunds.** When part of an order you paid for with credit is refunded, that part returns as credit. See the Returns, Refunds and Problems With Your Order page.
- **Gift cards.** The value of a gift card you redeem becomes credit. See the Gift Card Terms.
- **Referral rewards.** Rewards from the referral program are paid as credit. See the Referral Program Terms.
- **Plus membership.** Some membership benefits, such as credit back on delivered orders or a monthly credit, are paid as credit. See the Plus Membership Terms.
- **Decisions by our team.** Our team may add credit to settle a help request or as a goodwill gesture. A record of why is kept. In a help decision, our team may also send the card part of a refund to credit instead of to the card; only our team can do this, with a reason, and it is one way (see the Returns page).

## What you can do with it

You can use credit to buy products and services on AfroTransact, subject to these terms. At checkout, you choose whether to apply your credit; if the credit is less than the total, the rest is charged to your card or another payment method. [[LEGAL: Q12 confirm whether credit is applied by default or only when selected, and whether it can pay for gift cards, membership fees, delivery fees, taxes and services deposits]]

Credit is used atomically: if your order is placed, the credit is spent once and only once. If the order is cancelled before it ships, the credit comes back to your balance at once.

## What you can never do with it

**Store credit can never be converted to cash.** There is no cash-out, no payout, no refund of credit to a card or a bank account, and no way to transfer credit to another person, account or business. This is how the product works, and our staff have no option to change it.

Credit cannot be sold, bought, traded, pledged or exchanged, and it cannot be used for anything other than products and services on AfroTransact. [[LEGAL: Q08 and Q12 where state law requires cash back on small balances of gift cards or credit, we will follow it; counsel to list those states]]

## Refunds and credit

Refunds always go back to whoever paid, the way they paid. When you paid for an order part with credit and part with a card, the credit part returns as credit and the card part returns to the card. Customers cannot ask for the card part to come back as credit. Customers also cannot ask for credit to come back as card money. See the Returns page.

If credit was given for something that is later refunded, cancelled or reversed (for example credit back on an order that you then get refunded, or a referral reward for an order that is refunded), we may take back that credit. If you have already spent it, we may reduce credit you later receive, or we may ask you to pay the difference. ## Expiry and fees

Store credit does not expire and we do not charge fees for holding it, unless the law allows or requires otherwise. Certain promotional credit may have an end date, which we will show where it is given and before you accept it. [[LEGAL: Q08 and Q12 confirm. Promotional credit with an end date is a business option, not a product requirement; remove this sentence unless the business wants it]]

## Staff adjustments

Our team can add credit, and can correct or remove credit, for example to fix a mistake, to reverse a fraud or a payment dispute, or to carry out a legal duty. Each adjustment has a reason and is recorded with who made it. Larger adjustments need a second approval. You can ask us to explain any entry in your credit record.

## Fraud and misuse

We may freeze, reduce or cancel credit that we reasonably believe was obtained or used by fraud, abuse, a stolen or disputed payment, a breach of the Conditions of Use or the program terms (for example a referral abuse), or that is linked to a scam. We may also limit how much credit can be used in one order. We may close an account that does so, and we will tell you as the law requires.

## If you close your account

Before we close your account at your request, we will remind you of any credit. Credit cannot be turned into cash when the account closes. After an account closes, any remaining credit is lost, unless the law requires otherwise or we agree to restore it if you reopen the account within [[LEGAL: Q12 period during which credit is preserved after closure, for example 90 days]]. If we close your account for a reason other than a breach of our terms, we will give you a reasonable time to use your credit first.

## Deceased account holders

If an account holder dies, a representative may contact us at {legal}. Credit cannot be paid out in cash; we will discuss what we can do in line with the law. [[LEGAL: Q12 confirm approach]]

## Unclaimed property

Some state laws on unclaimed property may apply to unused credit. We will follow the law that applies and may need to report or transfer some balances. [[LEGAL: Q08 confirm]]

## Changes

We can change these terms for credit you receive in the future and will tell you before the change. We will not reduce a balance you already have, except as these terms say.
