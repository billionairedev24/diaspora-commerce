# Pre-order and Delivery Run Terms

## What these are

Delivery is expensive when it is one small order to a far place. To keep it fair, we group the orders of one area into one trip. There are two related offers.

- **Pre-order window.** A time window in which you can order products for a set delivery date. When the window closes, the paid orders are delivered, often on a delivery run.
- **Delivery run.** One planned trip by AfroTransact drivers that brings the orders of one area to their addresses or to pickup points, on a set date. A run is offered only for goods that are already in stock, and only in the places we have switched on.

Both are ordinary orders with a scheduled delivery. All the Conditions of Use and the Returns, Refunds and Problems With Your Order page apply to them, with the additions below. The offer you see at checkout shows the delivery date, the order cutoff, the fee and how many orders have been placed so far.

## Places we serve

We start in the US states and cities we have switched on. We offer a run or a window only where the address is inside a covered location. If your address is outside it, we ship by carrier instead, or tell you the order cannot be delivered. Where we serve changes over time. ## How you pay

You are charged like for any other order. At checkout, your card or payment method is authorised for the full amount. We collect the payment for each package when it leaves for you. If a package is cancelled before it leaves, the authorisation is released, so you are not charged; if the money was already collected, it is refunded. Pre-orders and delivery runs never ask for a second payment, and nothing is held back to be charged later. If your payment fails, we release your place in the run. Sales tax and any fee are shown before you place the order. See "Prices, payment and taxes" in the Conditions of Use for payment processing by Stripe.

## Order cutoff

Each window and run has a cutoff time. An order placed after the cutoff cannot join it. We show the next open run or window, and if none is open, we say so. An order placed just as the cutoff passes may be refused before you are charged.

## Minimum number of orders

Each window or run has a **minimum number of orders** that we set. We show it at checkout with honest progress, for example "7 of 10 orders so far", and we tell you that a run that does not reach its minimum may be cancelled and that you would be refunded in full.

- **If the minimum is reached,** the window or run goes ahead. It may close early once the minimum is reached.
- **If the end date passes without the minimum,** we make a decision: **cancel** (everyone is refunded) or **proceed** (the orders go ahead as planned). Our team decides, records who decided and why, and tells the customers. Proceeding may mean the delivery date moves, and we tell you if it does.
- **You can cancel** your own order until its shipment has been prepared, as described in the Returns page.

## If we cancel

If we cancel a pre-order window or a delivery run, **every order on it is refunded in full, including the delivery fee,** automatically, and the stock is released. If your card was only authorised, the authorisation is released instead, so you are whole either way. You do not have to ask. You will be told by the channels you chose (email, push, and SMS or WhatsApp if you opted in). Refunds appear on your statement according to your bank's timing. If you paid some of the order with store credit, that part returns as store credit. If only one package of a multi-package order is cancelled, the others are not changed.

## Delivery fees

The delivery fee depends on the delivery method. The rule that applies to you is shown at checkout, and the amount is recorded on your order. A later change in our fees never changes an order you already placed.

| Method | How the fee is set |
|---|---|
| **AfroTransact delivery** (our drivers) | A flat fee set by distance band from the run's location to your address. Addresses beyond the last band are shipped by carrier instead. |
| **Carrier shipping** (UPS, USPS, FedEx, DHL Express and others) | The carrier's rate for the order, or our handling or fallback fee. Some orders over a set value ship free. |
| **Pickup** (a pickup point, or pickup from the seller) | A pickup fee, which is often zero. |

A window or run can have its own door or pickup fee. A Plus membership can waive the delivery or shipping fee on orders over its minimum (see the Plus Membership Terms). Express options and pickup are not waived. Sales tax may apply to fees where the law says so. [[LEGAL: Q20 confirm]] Fee rules are set by us in our settings and can change; they are never hidden.

## Delivery dates and windows

The delivery date is our plan, and the delivery window (for example, morning or afternoon) is our best estimate. We do not promise a time. Weather, traffic, vehicle problems, a closed road, safety, the seller's stock or events outside our control may move a date. We tell you as soon as we can by your notification channels, and if the new date does not work, you can cancel the shipment if it has not been prepared. If we cannot deliver within a reasonable time after the date we gave, contact us through Account > Help. ## Delivery codes and proof of delivery

Each order that we deliver or that you collect has a **delivery code** (handover code) in your order page and your messages. **Give the code to the driver or seller only when your order is handed to you, and not before.** Do not share it with anyone else or read it out to anyone who contacts you. We will never ask you for your code by text, WhatsApp or phone, other than to the person handing you the package. The driver or seller uses the code to confirm that the right person has the package.

When a package is delivered we record **proof of delivery**: the delivery time and place, the code or the person who received it, and any photo or note that our driver takes. For a pickup, the code confirms that you collected it. We use these records to resolve a delivery problem, and they are covered by our Privacy Notice. If you ask someone else to receive the package, they need the code, and you are responsible for sharing it with them.

## Pickup points

A pickup point holds your package for the time shown. Bring the code and, if asked, ID. A package that is not collected in the time may be returned to us. Then we contact you; see "If nobody is there" below.

## If nobody is there to receive the delivery

If the driver cannot hand over your package, for example no one answers, access is not possible, the address is wrong, or the driver feels unsafe, the driver will try to reach you by call and text and wait a short time. If this fails we may reattempt on another day or at another window, ask you to collect from a pickup point, or return the package to us. We will tell you what we are doing and what, if anything, you are refunded, based on the reason. The delivery is treated as complete if the package is handed over to you or to the person you named or left in a safe place that you asked for, and you accept the risk of a leave-in-place instruction. Please keep your phone on and your delivery instructions up to date. If you paid and nothing was delivered because of us, we refund you.

## Perishable and frozen food

Many pre-orders and runs include fresh, chilled or frozen food. Please:

- Be ready to receive the package in the delivery window, and take it indoors and into the fridge or freezer promptly.
- Check the package when it arrives: look for spoilage, a broken seal, leaks, a strong smell, warm packaging or a temperature that does not seem right. If in doubt, do not eat it.
- Report a problem on the day of delivery through Account > Help with photos (see the Returns page). Keep the items and packaging until we reply unless unsafe.
- Allergens and ingredients are on the product label and listing. Check the label.

Perishable items are not accepted back by post, and you will not be asked to return them. Our team will decide each report. We will not deliver perishable items if we think they cannot arrive safely, and we may cancel and refund such items. ## Seller goods

For goods from independent sellers, the seller is the seller of the goods. Seller goods may reach you by AfroTransact fulfilment (stock already with us), by the seller's own delivery, by carrier, or by pickup. For a run, our driver may collect goods from sellers on the route before delivering. The seller is paid after delivery. This does not change your rights against the seller or us.

## Notices and messages

We announce new windows and runs, reminders of the cutoff, dispatch, confirmation and cancellation through the channels you chose. We send announcements by SMS or WhatsApp only if you opted in. Messages about your own order are order messages. See the SMS and Notification Terms.

## Changes

We may change how windows and runs work, the places, the fees and the minimums. A change never applies to an order you have already placed, except to fix a problem for you.
